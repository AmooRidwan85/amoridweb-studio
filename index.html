FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with Amorid Web Studio. Can’t find your answer? Reach out to us.

What is Amorid Web Studio?

Amorid Web Studio is a digital solutions studio helping businesses build and improve their online presence through web design, development, and related digital services.

Does Amorid Web Studio build custom software, not just websites?

Yes. We can create custom digital solutions tailored to your workflows and business requirements. Get in touch to discuss your project.

Do you develop mobile apps?

We can help plan and develop mobile app projects. Contact us with your requirements so we can recommend the right approach.

What technologies does Amorid Web Studio use?

We choose technologies based on each project’s goals, functionality, performance, and long-term maintenance needs.

Can Amorid Web Studio handle enterprise systems like ERP and CRM?

We can help businesses plan, build, or integrate systems such as ERP and CRM platforms. The best solution depends on your existing tools and requirements.

How much does a website cost in Lagos, Nigeria?

Website pricing depends on the project’s scope, features, content, and design requirements. Contact us for a quote tailored to your needs.

How long does it take to build a website?

The timeline depends on the size and complexity of the website, how quickly content and feedback are provided, and any custom features involved.

What does your website design service include?

Services can include project discovery, page structure, responsive design, development, testing, and launch support. The exact scope is agreed before work begins.

Can you build an online store (e-commerce website)?

Yes. We can build online stores with product pages, shopping cart, checkout, and other features suited to your business.

Do you offer SEO services in Lagos?

We can help improve your website’s search readiness and discuss SEO services based on your goals, audience, and current website.

Do you provide website hosting?

Hosting options can be discussed as part of your project. We’ll help you choose an option that fits your website’s needs.

Do you offer website management and maintenance?

Yes. Ongoing support and maintenance can help keep your website updated, secure, and working as expected.

Do you handle branding and logo design?

Branding and logo design can be included in a project or discussed as a separate service, depending on what your business needs.

Do you offer web design services outside Lagos?

Yes. We can work with clients outside Lagos and coordinate projects remotely.

What industries do you work with?

We work with businesses and organizations across a range of industries. Contact us to discuss your sector and project.

How do I get started with Amorid Web Studio?

Reach out with a brief description of your project, goals, and timeline. We’ll discuss your requirements and possible next steps.
